What Makes a Good Leaflet Design?

A good leaflet should communicate the main offer quickly and make it easy for customers to understand what the business provides. Clear headlines, readable text, strong contact details and a simple call to action can all help make the design easier to understand and use.

The design should also match the purpose of the campaign. A restaurant leaflet may focus on menus and special offers, while a local service business may highlight its main services, coverage area and contact details. Keeping the message focused helps avoid overcrowding the leaflet with unnecessary information.

Branding is also important. Your colours, logo, contact information and key message should work together so that customers can quickly recognise the business behind the leaflet. The layout should guide the reader naturally from the headline to the offer and then to the action you want them to take.

Choosing the Right Leaflet Size and Message

Different leaflet sizes can suit different campaigns. A6 leaflets are compact and useful for simple offers, while A5 provides more room for services, images and additional information. A4 can be useful when a campaign needs more detailed content or a larger visual layout.

Whatever size you choose, the most important information should be easy to find. Customers should be able to see what you offer, why it may be relevant to them and how they can contact you without having to search through too much text.
